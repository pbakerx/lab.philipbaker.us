  Every widget is a single self-contained HTML file — no libraries, no network, no
  storage — generated on request and run in a sandboxed frame. Download
  gives you the file; it works offline, anywhere. Your own session clears on reload —
  but anything you ♥ Favorite is kept, and everyone who visits sees it.
  Post to social gives a widget its own page and a link you can paste
  anywhere — the post unfurls into a card showing the widget, and on X the card is
  playable: the widget itself runs in the timeline. Want it moving before anyone taps?
  The share dialog records a short movie of you driving the widget and shares it — with
  your post text — straight to your apps; attached video autoplays in the feed.
