The Vault

In the late '90s I was part of the New Media "Lab" at Ackerman McQueen, building Shockwave and Flash games when a game in a browser was a small miracle. The plugins died; the work didn't. These are the original binaries, recovered from the Wayback Machine — running again through WebAssembly emulation, alongside the few other things worth keeping. The black-and-white site lives at /am-1998.

The games

Played in place through dirplayer (Shockwave/Director) and Ruffle (Flash). Emulation of 25-year-old plugins is imperfect — every card also serves the untouched original file.

Fragments & downloads

Pieces that survive but can't run in a browser: streaming-Shockwave ride stubs whose external casts were never archived, QuickTime VR tours, and the era's screensavers and installers — served as-is.

  • Darien Lake ride stubs (~830 bytes each) — Boomerang, Mind Eraser, Nightmare, Grizzly Run, Crocodile Isle, Hook's Lagoon, Popeye's Seaport, Lodge on the Lake
  • Brunswick preloaders (the '98 and Attitude ones now play in front of their games) — Attitude .dcr 9 KB, Bowling '98 .dcr 14 KB, Bowling '03 .dcr 25 KB
  • QuickTime VR — Streamwood bowling center .mov 252 KB, Frameworx tour .mov 252 KB
  • Brunswickids screensaver .exe 2.4 MB — download
  • WillTales — installer .exe 2.4 MB, Windows screensaver .zip 2.4 MB, Mac screensaver .sit.hqx 3 MB
  • DataTimes EyeQ upgrade .exe 2 MB — download

Every binary here was recovered from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine in August 2026 and is served unmodified. In-browser playback is emulation — dirplayer for Director/Shockwave, Ruffle for Flash — and some movies streamed assets that no longer exist, so not everything will run. The vintage executables are offered as historical artifacts; run them in an emulator, not on your actual machine.

The TV spots and venue films from these years now sit with the Ackerman McQueen page.
