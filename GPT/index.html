Pocket GPT

A real GPT, the same design as the chatbots you know, shrunk until you can watch every part of it work. It lives entirely in this page, starts out knowing nothing, and you'll teach it to write.

    💬 Chat

    Type anything and send it. It picks up where you leave off, one letter at a time, each one chosen from its own guesses.

    Ask it a question. It writes its answer one letter at a time and stops at the end of the line, the way every answer it has read ends. Chatting never teaches it anything: only training changes its numbers.

    Creativity

    Creativity (the pros call it temperature): low plays it safe and always takes its top guess, so it gets repetitive. High takes long shots, so it gets surprising, then weird, then nonsense.

    ⚙ Tune

    These change how it learns and apply instantly, even mid-training. No rebuild needed.

    Step size

    How big each nudge is (the learning rate). Too big and it overshoots: the loss jumps around or explodes.

    Batch

    Snippets read before each nudge. More gives a steadier direction, but each step is slower.

    Speed

    Steps per screen refresh. Changes how fast you watch, not what it learns.

    Optimizer

    ⬡ Rebuild

    The shape of its brain. A new shape means a brand-new, untrained brain: it has to learn from zero.

    Layers

    How many times it re-reads and rethinks before guessing.

    Heads

    Attention spotlights per layer.

    Width

    How many numbers describe each character inside it.

    Memory

    How many recent characters it can see at once (its context window).

    GPT-3 had 96 layers, 96 heads per layer, a width of 12,288 and 175 billion numbers. Modern chatbots remember hundreds of thousands of word-pieces at once.

    🧠 Saved brains

    Keep a brain you trained, come back to it later, or send it to a friend as a file.

    Saved brains live in this browser only. A downloaded file holds its numbers, its alphabet, its shape and the text it learned from, so anyone can load it here and carry on training where you left off.

      What you learned, in nine lines
      1. It only knows what it reads. Its whole world was one text.
      2. It starts as random numbers, which is why it babbled.
      3. Training is guess, check, nudge, thousands of times.
      4. Writing is one guess at a time, picked from letters like the ones you clicked.
      5. Creativity is how far down the list it's allowed to pick.
      6. Attention heads each look back for different clues, and switching one off shows what it was for.
      7. Learning is rolling downhill through a space with thousands of directions.
      8. Everything it knows is just numbers: no rules, no dictionary.
      9. A bigger brain is a new brain: it has to learn from zero.
      Everything runs in this page: no server, no API, no library. It's a real GPT (token and position embeddings, masked multi-head self-attention, an MLP, layer norm, residual connections), trained with hand-written backpropagation.
