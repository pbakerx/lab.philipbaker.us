# Acura A (300×600) — what we changed

Plain-language summary of the creative revisions, for client review.

- **Reworked the opening animation.** The repeated car/city images now fill
  the screen from the first frame and slowly rotate open into an offset
  "fan" (matching the social-video look) instead of building in one by one.
- **Scaled the fan down** so the full blue car is visible from the start.
- **Added a background fade at the top and bottom** (from the comp) — a soft
  darkening that makes the logo, headline, and button read clearly over the
  busy cityscape.
- **Headline is now vector artwork.** Converted the headline ("Precision
  Service For / Summer / Thrills / On Repeat.") from live text to SVG
  graphics — sharp at any size — and added the stark drop shadow from the
  comp. Sized and positioned the block to match the comp.
- **Removed embedded fonts** for the headline (it's a graphic now).
- **New crisp logo.** Vector (SVG) logo, sized slightly smaller per the comp.
- **Dealer copy is one consistent block** at **one fixed size for every
  dealer** — long names wrap instead of shrinking.

_The only live (dynamic) text in the ad is the dealer name and its intro
line. Everything else — headline, logo, the car fan — is now fixed artwork._

---

## 2026-06-02 — client review round 2

- **Acura logo now shows from the very first frame.** It used to fade in a
  moment after the ad started; it's now on screen immediately.
- **Front grille no longer cropped.** As the car fan grows and rotates, the
  front of the car (grille) was running off the right edge. We sized the
  whole fan down 3% and shifted it slightly left so the full grille stays in
  frame at rest — same animation feel, just framed better.
- **Car centered at the start.** The fan now begins with the blue car
  centered in frame, then subtly settles into its final framing (the same
  end position) as the cascade opens.
